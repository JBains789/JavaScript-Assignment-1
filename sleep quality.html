Sleep Quality

What is sleep quality?
Sleep quality is not how long you are asleep for but instead usually refers to the sleep efficiency (percent of time asleep) and sleep problems (fragmented or disrupted sleep, insomnia)[8].
How to improve sleep quality?

  • Have good sleep routine
  • Limit phone usage at night - avoid screens at least 1 hour before bed
  • Block out light
  • Avoid caffeine 6 hours before sleeping
  • Get exposure to natural light during the day
  • Exercise regularly
  • Manage stress and anxiety
  • Monitor what and when you eat - late night meals cause indigestoin

These are some ways that a you can improve your sleep [9]

Benefits of good sleep quality
According to Bupa, these are some of the following benefits of good sleep quality:

  • Improves attention and concentration
  • Learn and make memories
  • Helps you maintain a healthy weight
  • Blood Pressure Problems
  • Keep your heart healthy
  • Keep your immune strong
  • Looks after your emotional and mental well being
  • Reduce stress
  • Maintain Good Relationships

These are some benefits to having good quality of sleep both long term and short term provided by BUPA [10]
