Diet and Nutrition

What is Diet and Nutrition?
Diet is the food and drink that you routinely eat and drink.Nutrition is the nurtients in the food that you eat which are lipids, vitamins, proteins, carbohydrates and minerals.[5].
What is a healthy diet?
A helthy diet consists of food intake where the nutrients are balanced relative to the calorie intake. This will now show the healthy amount of each nutrient for a calorie intake of 2000 which is the recommended healthy amount for anyone aged above 10:[6].

According to WHO, these are the recommended amounts of intake for the following nutrients:

  • Carbohydrates - This primarily comes from whole grains, vegetables, fruits and pulses. Everyone above the age of 10 should aim for at least 25g of naturally occuring dietry fibre per day and 400g of fruits and vegetables.
     
  • Sugars - The consumption should be less than 10% of the daily energy intake which is about 50g.
     
  • Fats - Minimum consumption 15% of energy consumed per day up to 30%. No more than 10% energy intake should be saturated fats and no more than 15 energy intake should be trans fat.
     
  • Protein - approximately 50g-75g
     
  • Salts/Sodium and Potassium - Salt intake should be limited to lwss than 5g per day (2g of sodium per day). Potassium intake should be at least 3510mg per day.
     
Having a healthy diet plays a major role in good health because it can help protect yourself from malnutrition and non-communicable diseases.[6] It lets you have a stronger immune system and improves both your physical and mental health.
