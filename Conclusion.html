Conclusion
Looking after your health is very important and there are many ways to look after yourself by
improving on the factors that are already mentioned in this article however by not doing so, it
can lead to some serious health issues. These can be both short term and long term health issues
such as; Type 2 diabetes, poor muscle tone and posture, more falls in older people as well as reduced life expectancy.[12]
To reduce these harmful impacts on your health;eat a nutrient-dense diet, exercise regularly, maintain
healthy weight, manage stress effectively, stay hydrated, avoid tobacco products, limit alcohol intake, build strong
social connections and get good sleep. [11]
