Lifestyle and Health

Introduction

This website is desgined to provide you with information about your health and
how your lifestyle could be affecting your health. Health can be defined as the
general condition or state that your physical body or your mind is in. Lifestyle
is defined as the way in which a person does things and usually lives. Your lifestyle
can affect your health in many ways and by keeping helathy, this can help with your
mental well being, physical health, life expectancy and quality of life. This is for
anyone aged 11 or above as some complex words will be used.
